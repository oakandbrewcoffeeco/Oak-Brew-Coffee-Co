## Good coffee takes its time

An oak takes decades to grow, and a good roast takes patience too. We buy from farms we know, roast in small batches, and brew each cup to order.

Oak & Brew is a neighborhood coffee shop first. Pull up a chair, bring a book, stay a while.

Strong roots, slow roasts, and a seat for everyone.
